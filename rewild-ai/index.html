Safety by design

Verified accounts. Protected identity data.

Rewild AI is designed to prevent anonymous misuse. Every participant completes identity verification before receiving full platform access.

Identity verification

Users submit a government ID and selfie through a compliant KYC provider. Documents are processed securely and are not stored unnecessarily in the app database.

Business verification

Companies provide registration details and authorized-contact information. Verification status is displayed before they can publish listings or contact partners.

Location privacy

GPS is requested only with user consent. The platform should store approximate locations for matching, not continuously track users.

Secure communication

Chats occur inside the platform. Payment requests, illegal goods, threats, and off-platform contact attempts should be reportable and reviewable.
